# BoToBin3D: From Plastic to 3D Creativity ♻️🖨️

**Author:** Palma Orlando
**Project:** Dalla Plastica alla Creatività 3D (Circular Economy Project Work)

BoToBin3D is an innovative circular economy ecosystem designed for university campuses. The project addresses the massive environmental impact of plastic waste by collecting used plastic bottles and transforming them into sustainable filament for 3D printing. The name encapsulates the entire lifecycle of our mission: **Bo**ttle ➔ **To** ➔ **Bin** ➔ **3D**.

## 🌍 The Problem
* **High Consumption:** Italy is the 1st country in Europe and the world for bottled mineral water consumption, with 223 liters per capita annually. 
* **Recycling Deficit:** Out of 17.5 billion liters of non-alcoholic beverages consumed in plastic, approximately 7 billion PET bottles are not recycled each year (often incinerated, landfilled, or dispersed in the environment).
* **Market Opportunity:** The 3D printing market is expanding rapidly, reaching a value of $22 billion in 2023, and is projected to become a $70.8 billion industry by 2030.

## 🔄 The Circular Supply Chain
BoToBin3D creates a closed-loop system through four fundamental steps:

1. **Collection:** Users deposit bottles into Smart Eco-points. These bins are equipped with integrated solar panels and smart sensors to monitor fill levels and optimize pickup schedules, reducing transport CO₂ emissions.
2. **Sorting:** Collected bottles are re-inflated, sanitized, and sorted by color and material type (PET 1, HDPE 2, PP5) to ensure uniform filament quality.
3. **Transformation:** The bottles are cut into continuous strips, heated, and extruded through a die into a cylindrical 3D printing filament, which is then wound onto spools.
4. **Utilization:** The recycled PET filament is used to print durable, practical, and decorative 3D objects, reducing environmental waste.

## 📱 Mobile App Features (MVP)
The companion mobile app is designed to incentivize recycling through gamification and provide a seamless user experience.

* **Dashboard (Home):** Users can track the exact number of bottles they have recycled and the meters of filament produced. A unique QR code allows users to identify themselves at the eco-points to earn tokens.
* **Smart Map:** A satellite map locates nearby eco-points on campus, displaying real-time fill levels and scheduled emptying times for maximum convenience.
* **E-Commerce Marketplace:** A dedicated store where users can spend their earned tokens or use fiat currency to purchase 3D-printed creations (fashion, design, desk accessories) or discounted recycled filament.
* **User Profile & Impact:** Features a leaderboard to encourage friendly competition, a token balance tracker, and a live counter of the CO₂ emissions saved. It also provides transparent supply chain documentation.

## 📊 Market Validation
Customer validation surveys within the university demographic demonstrated strong product-market fit:
* **80%** of students expressed a willingness to use the smart eco-points.
* **64%** stated that their primary motivation to recycle would be earning rewards and discounts.
* Top requested 3D-printed rewards included custom gadgets (54.2%), desk accessories (45.8%), and home decor (45.8%).

## 🌱 Sustainable Development Goals (Agenda 2030)
BoToBin3D actively contributes to multiple UN SDGs:
* **Goal 9 (Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure):** Developing new, resilient technologies with recycled materials.
* **Goal 11 (Sustainable Cities and Communities):** Creating local recycling networks and raising environmental awareness on campus.
* **Goal 12 (Responsible Consumption and Production):** Reducing single-use plastics and the need to extract virgin raw materials.
* **Goal 13 (Climate Action):** Lowering CO₂ emissions by reducing the production and transport of new plastics.
* **Goal 14 (Life Below Water):** Preventing plastic waste from entering marine ecosystems.
* **Goal 17 (Partnerships for the Goals):** Collaborating with universities, recycling companies, and students to achieve common sustainability objectives[cite: 148, 155].
